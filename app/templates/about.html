About NU DentalConnect

A student project concept for coordinating procedure listings and appointment requests between patients and dentistry students.

Appointments, with a clear next step.

Dentistry students publish the procedures they need patients for. Patients review a listing, request a time, and see when the student confirms it or suggests another one.

Patient applications are also available for people who want to express interest before choosing an appointment time. An application and an appointment request are separate actions, and neither one books a visit on its own.

DentalConnect is an academic project concept. Campus authorization, service details, and clinical requirements should be confirmed with the institution before the platform is used for real appointments.

What the platform stores

Account details, procedure listings, appointment requests, and notifications. It does not store diagnoses, treatment notes, or medical records, so keep clinical information out of listing and application text.
