Good to know

Clear expectations make requests easier.

What an appointment listing is, and what happens after you send a request.

What is an appointment listing?

It describes a procedure a dentistry student needs a patient for, including the clinic location, preferred schedule, and who can take part.

Is my appointment booked when I request a time?

No. Your request is pending until the student confirms it. They may suggest another time for you to accept instead.

Can I contact a student without choosing a time?

Yes. You can send a patient application from any listing. An application does not book an appointment, and it stays separate from your requests.
